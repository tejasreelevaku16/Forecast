📐 How is the Forecast Trust Score Calculated?

1. Historical NWP Validation: WeatherTrust AI analyzes 3,500+ multi-year verification pairs comparing initial numerical forecasts with observed rainfall across Indian climatic zones.

2. 12 Predictive Features: Key signals include forecast lead time, squared lead decay, convective atmospheric index, run-to-run drift between consecutive model runs, and regional historical bust rates.

3. Probability Calibration: Predictions pass through 5-fold probability calibration so that a calculated 70% bust risk genuinely corresponds to 7 out of 10 historical forecasts busting.

4. Trust Score Formula: Trust Score = 100 - Calibrated Bust Probability. A score below 40 represents Low Confidence; 40–69 represents Moderate Confidence; 70+ represents Dependable Forecast.

ℹ️ About WeatherTrust AI

An explainable weather forecast reliability and bust-risk detection platform.

SIH 2024 Innovation

"Know the weather. Know when to trust it."

Normal weather applications answer: "What will the weather be?"
WeatherTrust AI answers: "How much should I trust this forecast?"

WeatherTrust AI acts as a dedicated forecast reliability and risk diagnosis layer on top of meteorological predictions. Rather than claiming to replace official meteorological forecasts from institutions like the India Meteorological Department (IMD), WeatherTrust AI audits forecast stability, models error likelihood, and explains uncertainty so decision-makers know when a forecast is dependable and when high-impact decisions should be deferred.
