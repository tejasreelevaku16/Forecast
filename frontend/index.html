About WeatherTrust AI

Normal weather applications answer: "What will the weather be?". WeatherTrust AI answers: "How much should I trust this forecast?"

WeatherTrust AI operates as an explainable reliability and risk diagnosis layer on top of meteorological predictions. By analyzing historical forecast-versus-observation discrepancies, forecast run-to-run drift, and regional atmospheric variability, the platform empowers decision makers to know when a forecast is dependable and when high-impact decisions should be deferred.
